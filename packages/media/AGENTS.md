There are some Big Buck Bunny assets in `tests/big-buck-bunny` for testing:

- `stream.m3u8` and `hls/*` are an HLS playlist, for testing HLS streaming
- `BigBuckBunny_320x180.mp4` is a small MP4 video file, for testing video playback
- `big-buck-bunny.webm` is an audio-only WebM for testing audio playback
- there are frame captures at 0:00, 0:01, 0:05, and 0:07.500 for testing frame-accurate playback
